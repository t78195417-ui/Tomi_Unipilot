Introduction & General Background

Department of Cyber Security

It is with great pleasure and a deep sense of responsibility that I welcome you all to the Department of Cyber Security. In an era where technology continues to evolve at an unprecedented pace, the importance of cybersecurity cannot be overstated. Cyber threats, data breaches, and digital vulnerabilities pose substantial risks to individuals, organisations, and nations. It is our collective duty to stay ahead of these threats through continuous learning, research, and innovation.

The supplied prospectus states that the Department of Cyber Security emerged with the Faculty of Computing in July 2024. The department aims to foster excellence, collaboration, and technological advancement through a blend of theoretical foundations and practical applications.

Students are encouraged to engage in research, participate in cybersecurity competitions, collaborate on innovative projects, and develop the skills needed to contribute to this evolving field.

Dr. D. A. Akinwumi
Ag. HOD, Cyber Security

General Overview

Recent developments in computing, network technologies, the internet and cloud technologies have increased the need for reliable and secure exchange of digital information. As the use of information technology expands, so do the potential consequences of cyber-attacks and the need for skilled professionals who can prevent and respond to them. Government agencies, businesses, industries and the military need qualified professionals to safeguard their systems, businesses and infrastructure.

Programme Philosophy & Objectives

What the Programme Aims to Develop

Programme focus

The programme aims to equip students with sufficient knowledge and skills to minimise and prevent cybersecurity threats and incidents. Students are also equipped to gather, analyse and present evidence of cybersecurity breaches professionally, and to identify and implement security practices that enhance the security of computers, computer-based systems and cyberspace.

Philosophy

The philosophy of the programme is to build capacity and develop human capital in cybersecurity to safeguard business transactions, corporate assets, critical infrastructure and cyber operations nationally and globally.

Objectives

  1. Produce graduates with foundational cybersecurity knowledge, skills and strategies to detect and prevent cyber-fraud.
  2. Enable graduates to analyse cybersecurity threats, attacks and risks and develop detection code and supportive software agents.
  3. Develop knowledge of cryptography, steganography and digital forensic science techniques for detecting cybercrimes.
  4. Develop critical thinking about cyber-intelligence security issues and cybersecurity strategies, drawing on case studies.
  5. Prepare graduates for self-employment, cybersecurity jobs and professional practice in government and industry.

Programme and duration

The programme offered is the Bachelor of Science Degree in Cyber Security.

  • UTME entry: minimum 4 years.
  • Direct Entry: minimum 3 years.
Admission

Admission Requirements

These requirements follow the supplied transcription. Confirm them with the current university admission notice before applying.

UTME Entry Requirements

  1. Five O'Level credit passes at SSCE (WAEC, GCE, NECO or NABTEB) in no more than two sittings, including English Language, Mathematics and Physics, plus two from Chemistry, Biology/Agricultural Science, Additional Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Studies, ICT or Basic Electronics.
  2. The transcript states that UTME subjects must include Mathematics, Physics and one of Biology, Chemistry, Agricultural Science, Economics or Geography, and also says “AND Computer Studies”. This wording is ambiguous in the source and should be checked against the official current requirements.
  3. Candidates successful in the science pre-degree programme of Adekunle Ajasin University, Akungba-Akoko may also be considered.

Source wording note: The prospectus heading for the minimum entry requirements refers to “B.Sc Degree in Computer Science” even though this is the Cyber Security section.

Direct Entry Requirements

The transcript describes students entering 200 level and lists these options, provided the stated O'Level requirements are met:

  1. Passes in two A-Level subjects, including Mathematics.
  2. At least Upper Credit in an ND in Computer Science, Statistics or Electronics/Electrical Engineering.
  3. NCE with at least Merit in Computer Science, Mathematics or Statistics may also be considered.

The source refers to the “Computer Science degree programme” in this section. Confirm the wording with the department.

Graduation

Graduation Requirements

  1. Pass all core courses, University and Faculty/School required courses, and electives.
  2. Accumulate at least 149 course units for students admitted through UTME or 117 course units for students admitted to 200 level through Direct Entry.
  3. Attain a minimum CGPA of 1.00.
  4. Be found worthy in character throughout studentship and complete the prescribed units from Core, Faculty and General Studies courses, as well as SIWES, Seminar and Final Year Project.
Registration for Courses

Course Categories & Registration Rules

Course categories

Compulsory courses: courses students must offer and pass before graduation.

Elective courses: departmental-prescribed courses that students register for and pass to meet University requirements before graduation.

Registration rules

  • Minimum workload: 15 units per semester.
  • Maximum workload: 28 units per semester.
  • A student may withdraw from a course within the Senate-stipulated period without penalty only with HOD approval and completion of an Add and Delete form.
  • Withdrawal without permission is treated as failure.
  • An examination taken for a course that was not registered will be cancelled.
